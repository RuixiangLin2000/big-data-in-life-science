# Course Plan

## Proposed modules

1. **Introduction** — definitions, scale, velocity, variety, and life-science use cases.
2. **Data ecosystem** — sequence, expression, imaging, clinical, sensor, and multi-omics data.
3. **Data engineering** — formats, metadata, databases, preprocessing, and quality control.
4. **Scalable analysis** — parallel computing, cloud concepts, workflow systems, and containers.
5. **Analytical methods** — statistical inference, dimensionality reduction, clustering, and prediction.
6. **Genomics applications** — variant analysis, transcriptomics, and single-cell analysis.
7. **Integration and translation** — multi-omics integration, clinical applications, and visualization.
8. **Responsible research** — reproducibility, privacy, bias, security, and ethics.

This plan is provisional and should be aligned with the official syllabus and lecture materials.
