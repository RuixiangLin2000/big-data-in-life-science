# Exam-Oriented Review

This page turns themes in the supplied example assessment into original study prompts and omits all personal or administrative information.

- Explain how the five Vs affect machine-learning design and validation.
- Describe the copy-in, compute, copy-out pattern for node-local temporary storage.
- Compare secure transfer, resumable synchronization, web download, compression, and archive tools.
- Trace a small MapReduce task and identify the cost of shuffling.
- Discuss scale, accuracy, interpretation, reproducibility, and privacy in genomics.
- Give an advantage and challenge of cloud computing and containers.
- Explain workflow dependency graphs, caching, logging, containers, and portability.
- Describe automated preprocessing, normalization, feature extraction, and modeling in high-content imaging.
- Interpret diverging training and validation curves and explain dropout.

For each topic, practice a definition, a life-science example, a tradeoff, and one failure mode.
